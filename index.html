Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 360 panorama viewer?

A 360 panorama viewer is an interactive web tool that projects a 2:1 equirectangular image onto the inside surface of a virtual 3D sphere. Using WebGL hardware acceleration, it simulates standing inside the environment, allowing you to look in all directions (360 degrees horizontally and 180 degrees vertically) with complete freedom.

Does viewing or converting require uploading photos to a server?

No. All viewing, cubemap slicing, globe projection, and metadata injection are executed 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebGL, and JavaScript TypedArrays. Your private photos never leave your device.

What image formats and resolutions are supported?

The viewer supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, and 32-bit Radiance .HDR files in standard 2:1 equirectangular aspect ratios, ranging from 1024×512 preview resolutions up to 8192×4096 and 16K ultra-high-resolution panoramas.

Can I view panoramas on mobile smartphones and VR headsets?

Yes. The viewer is fully responsive on iOS Safari and Android Chrome, supporting touch drag and pinch-to-zoom. It also features a Cardboard VR stereo split-screen mode that works with Google Cardboard and mobile VR phone holders.
