Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how 360 Panorama Viewer ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Data Collection & Processing Boundaries

  • Local Processing: Viewing files and using the cubemap generator or metadata injector process those files in your browser. Creating a share link uploads the image to our service for storage and access through that link.
  • Shared Images: Anyone with a share link can access the shared image, even if it is not listed in the public showcase. You can revoke a share link from your account.
  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your email address, display name, and a cryptographic hash of your password. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • AI Generation Data: When you submit a prompt to generate an AI panorama or upload an optional reference image, that data is processed solely by our backend pipeline to fulfill your generation request.
  • Payment Records: Payments are processed directly through PayPal. We store transaction IDs, subscription status, and invoice amounts necessary to credit your account; we never store your payment card or bank credentials.

2. Third-Party Infrastructure

We use reputable third-party infrastructure to deliver the service reliably and securely:

  • Cloudflare: Provides edge hosting and Cloudflare Turnstile anti-bot verification during account registration to protect our systems against automated abuse.
  • Resend: Used exclusively to deliver transactional account emails, such as email verification links.
  • PayPal: Handles subscription payments, renewals, and invoice processing.
  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand site usage and improve the service. It collects page views and usage events, such as registration or login, generation results, and checkout initiation, as well as device and browser information. Google may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about its data practices.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage for service functions and preferences, including:

  • Maintaining your authenticated session token.
  • Remembering your interface language preference.
  • Remembering guest account data and certain service or administrative settings.

Local storage can remain on your device after you close the browser until it is cleared. We also use session storage for some temporary service data. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies as described above.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Account records and transaction history are retained for the duration of your account's lifecycle to maintain your credit balance and fulfill legal accounting requirements.

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. To request account deletion, email support@360panoramaviewer.com from your registered address.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, contact us at support@360panoramaviewer.com.
