How to Replace Your Minecraft Main Menu Background

  1. Upload or paste any 360° equirectangular panorama.
  2. Click Minecraft Pack (ZIP) to download a formatted resource pack.
  3. Place the downloaded ZIP file into your .minecraft/resourcepacks/ directory.
  4. In Minecraft, go to Options → Resource Packs and enable your custom panorama pack.

Frequently Asked Questions

What files are required for a Minecraft title screen panorama?

Minecraft uses six cube face images: panorama_0.png (South), panorama_1.png (West), panorama_2.png (North), panorama_3.png (East), panorama_4.png (Up), and panorama_5.png (Down). Our tool automatically slices and packages them with pack.mcmeta.

Which Minecraft editions and versions are supported?

The exported resource pack works with Minecraft Java Edition 1.13 through 1.21+. The six PNGs can also be extracted for Bedrock Edition or older legacy versions.

Are my panorama images uploaded to a server?

No. Slicing and ZIP generation run entirely on your device with local WebGL rendering. Zero upload or signup needed.
