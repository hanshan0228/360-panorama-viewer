1. Service Overview & Privacy Boundaries

360 Panorama Viewer provides browser-based spherical panorama viewing, conversion tools (including equirectangular to cubemap and globe projection), and AI-assisted panorama generation.

  • Client-Side Tools: The standard 360 viewer, cubemap slicer, and metadata injector run locally in your web browser. Your local image files are rendered in client memory and are never uploaded to our servers.
