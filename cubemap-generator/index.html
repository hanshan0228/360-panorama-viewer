How to Convert an Equirectangular Panorama to a Cubemap

  2. Select Face Resolution: Choose your output resolution per face: 512px, 1024px, 2048px, or native 4096px per square face.
  3. Mathematical Inverse Tangent Projection: Our client-side WebGL shader calculates exact spherical-to-planar ray intersections for all six orthogonal directions (+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z) with zero optical distortion.
  4. Download Batch ZIP Archive: Package all six cube faces into an organized ZIP file named according to Unity and Three.js conventions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What naming convention does the downloaded ZIP file use?

The archive contains six individual PNG images named posx.png (+X Right), negx.png (-X Left), posy.png (+Y Up), negy.png (-Y Down), posz.png (+Z Front), and negz.png (-Z Back), ensuring seamless drag-and-drop into Unity and Three.js asset inspectors.

Are my photos uploaded or stored on any server?

No. Slicing is performed locally in your browser memory using HTML5 Canvas and WebGL shader math. No data is sent over the network.

What source image formats work best?

Any 2:1 aspect ratio equirectangular panorama in PNG, JPG, JPEG, or WebP format. Standard resolutions include 2048×1024, 4096×2048, and 8192×4096.

Step-by-Step Game Engine Cubemap Setup

After exporting your six cube faces (+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z) in the ZIP archive, configure them in your preferred 3D engine:

  • Unity 6-Sided Skybox Material: Create a new Material in your Assets folder. Set the Shader to Skybox > 6 Sided. Drag posz.png to Front (+Z), negz.png to Back (-Z), negx.png to Left (-X), posx.png to Right (+X), posy.png to Up (+Y), and negy.png to Down (-Y).
  • Unreal Engine 5 SkyAtmosphere & CubeTexture: Import the six faces, create a CubeTexture asset, and assign it to your Skylight or custom post-process skybox volume.
  • Three.js CubeTextureLoader: Load the six faces with new THREE.CubeTextureLoader().load(['posx.png', 'negx.png', 'posy.png', 'negy.png', 'posz.png', 'negz.png']) and assign to scene.background.
  • Godot 4 PanoramaSkyMaterial: Godot supports direct cubemap and panorama textures in WorldEnvironment for instant 360-degree ambient illumination.
