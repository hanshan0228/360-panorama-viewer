Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 360 panorama showcase?

A showcase is a curated public collection of real 2:1 equirectangular panoramas, AI-generated skyboxes, and HDRI environment maps published by community members to study spatial composition, lighting, and seamless boundary wrapping.

Are the panoramas in the showcase free to view?

Yes. Every showcase entry opens in our free online 360 Panorama Viewer with smooth damped inertia panning, Little Planet projection, and VR cardboard mode without needing an account or app installation.

What resolution and aspect ratio are supported?

Panoramas should use a standard 2:1 width-to-height equirectangular aspect ratio (such as 2048×1024, 4096×2048, or 8192×4096) in PNG or JPEG format.

Studying Spatial Composition in 360° Environments

Browsing community showcases offers unique educational value for 3D level designers, architectural photographers, and digital artists:

  • Horizon Placement & Eyeline Balance: See how top creators ground the horizon line to prevent user motion sickness in VR headsets.
  • Zenith and Nadir Management: Study how artists blend straight-up celestial textures and straight-down ground surfaces to avoid stretching artifacts.
  • Seam Quality Inspection: Rotate 360 degrees to inspect where the left and right borders join, observing how multi-band gradient healing eliminates visible cuts.
  • Light Direction and Specular Bounces: Analyze primary illumination angles, key light contrast, and ambient fill in high dynamic range environments.
