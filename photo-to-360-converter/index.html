Advanced Spatial Outpainting Pipeline

Traditional image outpainting works on flat Cartesian coordinates, producing curved distortion when wrapped onto a sphere. 360 Panorama Viewer uses a spherical generative architecture trained on 360×180 degree coordinate spaces:

  • Spherical Latitude-Longitude Grid Awareness: Models polar pinching at zenith (+90°) and nadir (-90°) so skies and floors converge naturally without squashing.
  • Continuous Wrap Border Alignment: The left (0°) and right (360°) borders are evaluated jointly during diffusion sampling, guaranteeing perfect edge matching.
  • Architectural Horizon Leveling: Gravity-aligned horizon cues keep indoor floorboards, ceilings, and walls parallel to virtual world coordinates.
  • Instant Client Inspection: Verify the spatial expansion in interactive WebGL immediately without server rendering delay.
