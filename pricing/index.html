360° Panorama Credits & Pricing

The AI generator runs on credits; the viewer and every local converter are free. Prices below are in US dollars, and the checkout page shows the exact charge before you approve it.

Why the tools are split into free and paid

Rendering, slicing, projecting and metadata editing happen inside your browser, so they cost the service almost nothing and stay free without an account. AI generation, by contrast, calls a paid image model for every attempt, so it is billed in credits by output resolution.

What every paid option includes

  • 1K, 2K and 4K output options, subject to model limits.
  • PNG download plus in-browser cubemap and Radiance .HDR export.
  • Commercial use of panoramas generated with paid credits.
  • Automatic release of reserved credits when a generation fails quality review twice or the upstream model fails.
  • One-click cancellation of automatic renewal; purchased credits stay on the account.

Cost per image, in practice

At the Pro Creator rate, a 2K generation works out to roughly $0.17 and a 4K generation to roughly $0.33. One-time packs cost more per credit, but they never expire and carry no renewal. Choose packs for a single project and a subscription for recurring work.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need credits to view or convert a panorama?

No. Viewing, cubemap slicing, cubemap-to-equirectangular conversion, HDRI tone mapping, metadata injection and panorama-to-video export run locally in your browser and cost nothing.

How many credits does one AI panorama cost?

One credit for 1K output, two for 2K and four for 4K. Failed generations release their reserved credits.

Do one-time pack credits expire?

Purchased one-time credits can be used at any time and do not renew. Subscription credits are added after each confirmed payment.

Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes. Open Manage subscription in your account to stop automatic renewal. Credits already purchased stay on the account.

Which payment methods are supported?

Checkout is completed on PayPal, which accepts a PayPal balance or a card where guest checkout is available. The final amount is shown before payment.

Is there an API or team plan?

Public read endpoints need no key. Generation requires an account session. For team seats, invoicing or an API plan, contact us with your expected volume.
