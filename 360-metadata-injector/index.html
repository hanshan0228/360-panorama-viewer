Understanding PhotoSphere GPano XMP Tags

Google developed the Photo Sphere XMP metadata specification to allow standard JPEG image files to encapsulate complete 360-degree spherical metadata. Platforms like Facebook, Google Maps, Kuula, and VR photo viewers parse these specific XML tags inside the APP1 segment to determine projection math, field of view, and crop bounds:

  • UsePanoramaViewer=True: Signals to social feed algorithms that the photo should be initialized in an interactive 3D WebGL spherical panorama viewer instead of a standard 2D flat image view.
  • ProjectionType=equirectangular: Instructs the 3D graphics engine to wrap the pixel rectangular plane using spherical longitude-latitude geometry.
  • FullPanoWidthPixels & FullPanoHeightPixels: Defines the dimensions of the full theoretical sphere (standard 2:1 ratio, such as 4096×2048 or 8192×4096).
  • CroppedAreaImageWidthPixels & HeightPixels: Specifies the active pixel canvas dimensions if the image was cropped or captured as a partial cylindrical panorama.
  • CroppedAreaLeftPixels & TopPixels: Sets positional offsets ensuring horizons remain level and the center point is accurately oriented.
