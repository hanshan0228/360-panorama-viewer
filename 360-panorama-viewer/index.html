What is a panorama viewer?

A panorama viewer (also called a panoramic viewer, 360 viewer, or 360 photo viewer) wraps a flat 2:1 equirectangular photo around the inside of a virtual sphere, so dragging the image feels like standing inside the scene. It is the same projection Google Street View, Facebook 360 photos, and VR headsets use.

How to view a 360° panorama online

  1. Load the photo: drag and drop your equirectangular image, click Open Local Panorama, or press Ctrl + V to paste from the clipboard.
  2. Look around: drag with the mouse, swipe on mobile, or use the arrow keys to pan 360° horizontally and 180° vertically.
  3. Switch the projection: 360° Sphere for interior roaming, Little Planet for a stereographic fisheye shot, VR Split for a cardboard headset.
  4. Reuse the result: export a PNG snapshot, share a copy by link, or slice the image into a 6-face cubemap. Embedding requires a hosted image.

Supported formats, resolutions, and 360 cameras

2:1 equirectangular JPG, PNG, and WebP images open directly. Supported image dimensions depend on your device and browser. Non-2:1 photos may look stretched in a spherical viewer.

Cameras and apps that work: Insta360 ONE X / X2 / X3 / X4, DJI (Air, Mavic, and Mini 360 sphere panoramas), GoPro MAX, Ricoh Theta S / V / Z1, Kandao QooCam, Trisio, iPhone and Android panorama mode, and Google Street View photo exports — choose the equirectangular JPG export. Insta360 .insp raws, dual-fisheye DNGs, and HEIC stills must be stitched or converted to JPG first.

Frequently asked questions

Are my 360° photos uploaded or stored on any server?

Local preview runs in your browser without uploading your photo. If you choose to create a share link, a copy is uploaded so people with the link can view it.

What image formats and resolutions are supported?

Standard 2:1 equirectangular panoramas in PNG, JPEG, and WebP. Maximum usable dimensions depend on the browser and device. Exporting an ordinary image as Radiance RGBE .HDR does not create measured high-dynamic-range lighting data.

Is a panorama viewer the same thing as a panoramic viewer or 360 viewer?

Yes — panorama viewer, panoramic viewer, 360 viewer, and 360 photo viewer all describe the same tool: a player that renders a 2:1 equirectangular photo inside an interactive sphere you can rotate and zoom.

Can I convert the 360° panorama into game engine cubemaps?

Yes. Send the panorama into the built-in Cubemap Generator to slice it into 6 faces (posx, negx, posy, negy, posz, negz) for Unity, Unreal Engine 5, or Godot.
