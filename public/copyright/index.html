Copyright reports

To report a copyright concern about material hosted by 360 Panorama Viewer, email support@360panoramaviewer.com.

Information to include

  • Your name and contact information, including a mailing address, telephone number and email address.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list if your report covers several works.
  • The exact URLs of the material you are reporting, with enough detail for us to locate it.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the exclusive right allegedly infringed.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

If your content was removed and you believe this was a mistake, contact the same address with the affected URLs and the reason you dispute the removal.

Accounts and shared content

You must have the rights needed to upload or share material through this service. We may remove infringing material and restrict or terminate accounts of repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances.
