How to Replace Your Minecraft Main Menu Background

  1. Upload or paste any 360° equirectangular panorama.
  2. Click Minecraft Pack (ZIP) to download a formatted resource pack.
  3. Place the downloaded ZIP file into your .minecraft/resourcepacks/ directory.
  4. In Minecraft, go to Options → Resource Packs and enable your custom panorama pack.
