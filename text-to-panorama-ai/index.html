How to Write a 360° Panorama Prompt

  3. Materials and detail: "wet asphalt reflections", "oak floor", "carved stone".

AI Text to Panorama vs 360° Camera vs Photo Stitching

  • AI text to panorama: seconds, no equipment, and impossible scenes such as fantasy or deep space are possible.
  • 360° camera: one to three hours on location, the most physically accurate result, but only real places can be captured.
  • DSLR photo stitching: twenty to sixty minutes of shooting and stitching, and it needs a tripod and software licences.
  • Full 3D render: maximum control at the cost of days of modelling work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes prompt engineering for 360° panoramas unique?

Unlike standard 2D image prompts that describe a single focused camera perspective, 360 panorama prompts must establish an entire 360-degree environment sphere. Mentioning horizon details, ceiling or sky textures, floor surfaces, and lighting direction ensures the AI model populates every angle cohesively without blank or distorted polar voids.

How does the AI ensure the panorama wraps seamlessly?

Our diffusion pipeline incorporates multi-band Poisson harmonic boundary blending that matches edge luminance and color gradients between the 0-degree and 360-degree meridians, producing an undetectable wraparound transition.

Can I slice text-generated panoramas into cubemaps or HDRI files?

Yes. Once generated, any panorama can be sliced into 6 rectilinear cube faces for Unity or Unreal Engine, or exported as a 32-bit Radiance .HDR environment map with one click.
