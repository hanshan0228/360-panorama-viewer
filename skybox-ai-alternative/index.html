Skybox AI Alternative: What Actually Fits Your Project

"Skybox AI alternative" usually means one of three different jobs: generating a new environment, downloading an existing HDRI, or converting a panorama you already own into engine-ready cube faces. Pick the right category first — the pricing models differ completely.

By 360 Panorama Viewer · Updated 16 November 2026 · 6 minute read

What stays free here

  • Viewing: open a 2:1 equirectangular panorama in WebGL with no upload and no account.
  • Cubemap slicing: export posx, negx, posy, negy, posz and negz PNGs at 256, 512 or 1024 pixels per face.
  • HDRI handling: open a Radiance .hdr environment, adjust exposure and export tone-mapped cube faces.
  • Reverse conversion: rebuild a 2:1 panorama from six cube faces.

None of these call a server model, so none of them consume credits.

When paying per generation beats subscribing

A subscription makes sense when you generate every week and want a fixed cost. Credits make sense when you generate in bursts — a game jam, one architecture pitch, a handful of storefront scenes — and then stop for months.

Here, one-time packs start at $4.99 for 25 credits, and credits do not expire. A 2K generation costs two credits and a 4K generation costs four, so a pack covers a small project without a recurring charge. Full plan details are on the pricing page.

What AI generation can and cannot do

These models create a new image that is styled after your prompt or reference photo. They do not reconstruct the real room behind your camera, and they cannot guarantee consistent geometry across separate generations. If a scene must match a real location, use captured HDRIs or your own panorama and slice it locally.

Exporting an ordinary 8-bit image as a Radiance .HDR file stores it in an HDR container; it does not recover lighting information the source never captured.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to Skybox AI?

It depends on the job. Free CC0 HDRI libraries cost nothing but are not generated for your scene. This site keeps viewing, cubemap slicing and HDRI handling free, while AI generation uses credits instead of a mandatory subscription.

Can I use an existing panorama as a skybox without AI?

Yes. Load a 2:1 equirectangular panorama and slice it into six cube faces locally. Nothing is uploaded and no credits are used.

Do I need a subscription to generate a skybox here?

No. One-time credit packs are available and their credits do not expire. Subscriptions are optional and exist for recurring work.

Do generated skyboxes work in Unity, Unreal Engine and Three.js?

Export a 2:1 equirectangular PNG for skybox shaders, or six PNG faces named posx, negx, posy, negy, posz and negz for six-sided skyboxes. Unreal's HDRI Backdrop expects a Radiance .hdr file; a PNG does not carry HDR lighting values.
