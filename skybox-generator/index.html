How do I import the skybox into Unity?

In Unity, create a new Material and set its Shader to Skybox > 6 Sided (for sliced cube faces) or Skybox > Panoramic (for the 2:1 equirectangular PNG). Assign the texture maps and set the material under Window > Rendering > Lighting > Environment Skybox.
